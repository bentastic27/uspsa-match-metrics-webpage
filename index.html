USPSA Accuracy Breakdown

Load a PractiScore results export (.txt) to break down A/B/C/D/Miss/No Shoot by division and class. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Drop the export here, or choose a file

Penalties = procedurals, double popper misses, late/extra shots, extra hits and additional penalties (Misses and No Shoots are separate columns). "Avg per shooter-stage" divides by the number of shooter-stage scores in the row. The stage charts show one bar per stage, each scaled to 100% of its A/C/D/Miss/No Shoot/Penalty total (hover a color for the exact count, with a table of every zone under each chart; untick a zone in the legend to hide it and rescale): all classes combined, then each class; the Class dropdown only applies to the counts tab. DQ/DNF shooters and empty scores are excluded; chrono stages are ignored.
